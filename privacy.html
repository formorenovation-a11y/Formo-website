When you request an estimate, request a call back, or otherwise contact us, we may collect the information you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, city or service location, and details about your project.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, the pages you view, and the dates and times of your visits.

Information from third parties

We may receive information about your interactions with our advertisements from advertising and analytics providers such as Google.

  • To measure the performance of our advertising and understand how visitors use the Site;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements; and
  • To protect the rights, property, and safety of Formo Renovation, our customers, and others.

We and our service providers use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your preferences, and measure and improve our advertising. For example, we use Google Ads conversion tracking and related tools to understand which ads lead to inquiries.

You can usually set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; however, some parts of the Site may not function properly without them. You can learn about and opt out of certain interest-based advertising at optout.aboutads.info and google.com/settings/ads.
